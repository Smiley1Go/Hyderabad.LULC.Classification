Project overview

What this map shows

A supervised Random Forest classification separating Hyderabad's surface into five land-cover classes — water, built-up, vegetation, cropland, and bare land — built from a cloud-filtered Sentinel-2 composite.

Interpretation & limitations

Built-up land dominates Hyderabad's footprint at just over half the mapped area, with vegetation and bare land trailing behind. Built-up and bare land are the most confused pair — dry-season exposed soil, construction sites, and bright rooftops share similar reflectance. Vegetation and cropland also blur together once seasonal crops senesce toward the end of the dry window. Overall accuracy (82%) clears the target threshold, but bare-land recall stays weak — a wetter-season composite, more training samples in the confused classes, or a boosted-tree classifier would likely tighten this further.
